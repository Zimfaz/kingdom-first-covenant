KINGDOM FIRSTCovenant

Why build it

For Christians who hope to marry, not to browse.

Most dating apps do well when people keep scrolling. This one does well when people leave married.

So it moves slowly on purpose: faith first, faces second, and never a conversation alone.

Marriage is a good gift from God. So is a single life lived for Him.

What you won’t find here

Swiping, likes or “who viewed you”

Match percentages or scores

Endless browsing or a feed

Streaks, read receipts or “last active”

Paying to be seen first

The behavioural reviewer’s point: an app that earns more when people stay single has the wrong incentives. Everything here is designed so a good week can mean not opening the app at all.
