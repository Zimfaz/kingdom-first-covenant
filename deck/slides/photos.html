Tier 2

Photos only after two yeses

When you both say yes to each other’s faith summary, you see each other’s photos at the same moment.

Up to three photos, no filters. The first is checked against the person’s ID selfie.

Then each of you invites a companion, and the conversation can begin.

The photos in the prototype are placeholders. In the real app nobody can browse photos; they appear only inside an introduction both people have said yes to.
