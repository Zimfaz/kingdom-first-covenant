Companions

Never a conversation alone

Each of you invites a companion: a parent, brother or sister, friend, priest or pastor. All four of you see every message.

Companions promise to keep things private and never push either way. They can write, pause and report, but they can’t decide anything for you.

You can change your companion quietly, at any time.

Phone numbers, emails and links are blocked in messages at first, which stops most scams moving people off the app. Companions pass the same ID check and can’t be looking for marriage on the app themselves.
