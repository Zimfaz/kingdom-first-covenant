Closing an introduction kindly

You never have to give a reason. You can send one of two gentle messages, or none.

The other person hears that it says nothing about their worth to God. Two people who close an introduction aren’t introduced again unless both ask.

Engagements are never announced or counted, so nobody still waiting feels behind.

No free-text messages when closing: someone who has just been turned down shouldn’t receive hurtful words.
