Tier 1

Faith and marriage questions first

Five short groups: your faith and church, the Church and the sacraments, marriage itself, children and family, and everyday life.

Mark a few answers “This matters a lot to me”. Those must agree before anyone is introduced; the rest only help order introductions.

Answers use each church’s own words. Nobody ever sees a score or a percentage.

Examples: how you see Holy Communion, Mary and the saints, leadership in marriage, children and family planning, how involved families should be. There’s always “Skip for now” and “I’d rather not say”.
