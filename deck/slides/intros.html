Introductions

A few at a time, in words

Up to three introductions arrive on Thursday evening. Never on a Sunday, and no browsing in between.

Each one shows “What you share” and “Things to talk about”, in the person’s own words. No photos yet.

Your yes is private. A conversation opens only if you both say yes; otherwise nobody learns who said no.

One conversation is open at a time, with at most one more introduction waiting. That keeps people from juggling several at once.
