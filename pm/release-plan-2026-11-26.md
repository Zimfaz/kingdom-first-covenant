## 4. Risks that could push the dates
1. Equality Act advice forces a redesign (back to September).
2. Apple refuses under 4.3(b): Android launches; iPhone follows the appeal.
3. DPIA needs ICO prior consultation (adds about 8 weeks).
4. The ID provider declines or is slow.
5. No second moderator: the closed test waits.
6. Too few people per church family: open areas only when targets are met.
7. KFL slips: KFL wins, Covenant moves.
8. Solicitor capacity over Christmas.

## 5. What we never cut to go faster
- A Companion for each person in every conversation, enforced on the server.
- ID check and live selfie for members **and** Companions; 18+ from the document.
- A person reviews every profile and photo before anyone sees it.
- The private safety line, the forced-marriage route (never contact the family) and same-day review.
- DPIA, risk assessments and solicitor sign-off before any real faith, story or photo is entered.
- Two trained moderators and the safeguarding advisers before the closed test.
- Safeguards kept for 6 months after engagement; real deletion in the app.
- The pastoral never-say list and a reader from each tradition.
- Kingdom First Life first each day; no Covenant work 4–14 Dec.
