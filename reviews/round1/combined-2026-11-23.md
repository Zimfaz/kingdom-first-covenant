# Kingdom First Covenant: round 1 combined review (23 Nov 2026)

Six reviewers: pastoral, behavioural, UX, product, marketing, brand (files in this folder). Brief: docs/brief-2026-11-23.md. **Nothing is built until the owner approves.**

## Agreed by all or nearly all
- **Words shape behaviour.** "Introduction", never "match", "swipe", "unmatch", "date night", "hook up"; "divorcee" never used as a label. No percentages or scores on screen: "What you share" and "Things to talk about".
- **Slow by design.** A few introductions on one fixed weekday evening (never Sunday), no browsing, search, swiping, feeds, likes counts, streaks, read receipts, timers or paying to be seen. One open conversation at a time.
- **Tier 1 first, in words.** About 30–35 questions in groups (faith and church, sacraments, marriage, children, everyday life), one group a sitting, saved as you go; progress in words. Each person marks a few must-agree answers (pastoral: up to 5; product: up to 8). Matching never uses looks or popularity.
- **Two quiet yeses.** Each person says yes privately; a conversation opens only on two yeses; otherwise both see "This introduction has closed" and nobody learns who said no.
- **Photos only when both choose**, both see them at once, up to 3, no filters, first one checked against the ID selfie.
- **Chaperones:** each person brings their own (behavioural, pastoral, product); chaperones pass the same ID check, can read, write, pause and report, but can't approve, veto, close or message anyone privately; the member can change chaperone silently; with no chaperone present the conversation pauses, never becomes one-to-one. Text only; numbers, emails, links and addresses filtered.
- **"I feel pressured / I'm worried about my safety"**: a private line no chaperone or match can see, leading to UK help (domestic abuse lines for all four nations, Forced Marriage Unit, Karma Nirvana, Report Fraud) and same-day review. The most important safeguard.
- **Marital history** (pastoral, verified): never married, widowed, divorced, declared null, nullity in process. Each person chooses who they're open to; nothing pre-ticked; kind explanations per church (Catholic: no Catholic wedding after divorce until the first marriage is declared null; Orthodox: economia; Church of England: at the priest's discretion). No documents collected.
- **Kind endings** in about 3 taps, closing words from a checked set, no reasons. Engagements need both to confirm, are never announced or counted; marrying closes both accounts.
- **Verified adults only:** ID document plus live selfie for every member and chaperone (Stripe Identity about £1.25 a check; we pay, safety is never charged).
- **Law before launch:** Online Safety Act risk assessments, reporting tools, children's access assessment; UK GDPR explicit consent and DPIA (religion, marital history, photos, implied orientation); **Equality Act**: the religious exception does not cover a commercial company, so the solicitor must advise on Christians-only and man–woman-only introductions before launch.
- **People:** a second trained moderator and an independent safeguarding adviser before beta.
- **Brand (proposal):** "KINGDOM FIRST *Covenant*"; icon A (crown with two joined rings, gold base bar); new signature wine #7B2D3B (light wine #E4A6A0 on dark); never "KFC"; strap line "Introduced for marriage."; trade mark in classes 9, 42 and 45.
- **Timing:** prototype in December; build after the KFL January update; London waitlist after Easter, closed beta summer 2027, UK launch September 2027.

## Where reviewers differ (owner decides)
1. **When photos are shared:** after both say yes to the Tier 1 summary, before talking (UX, product) or after the chaperoned conversation has started, before meeting (behavioural).
2. **How many introductions:** one a week (product) or up to three on Thursday evening (UX); at most one waiting while a conversation is open (behavioural).
3. **Price:** membership £14.99 a month or £59.99 for 6 months needed to say yes (product); free to message, membership for extras (marketing); a one-off or per-season price instead of monthly so the app never earns more from people staying single (behavioural).
4. **Chaperone name:** keep "chaperone" or a warmer word (marketing).
