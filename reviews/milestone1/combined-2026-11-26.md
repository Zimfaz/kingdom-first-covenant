# Covenant milestone 1: combined review (26 Nov 2026)

Eight reviewers looked at milestone 1: pastoral, UX, UI, behavioural, product, brand, copy and marketing. The two store reviews (reviews/store/apple-4-3-review-2026-11-26.md and the comparison in reviews/comparison/) are inputs to this summary. Each reviewer's full file is in this folder.

## Fixed the same night (26 Nov)
- **Saved answers can't be mixed up when questions change.** Answers are saved by fixed question and option ids (`OPTION_IDS` in src/lib/questions.ts), never by position. *(product, UX)*
- **A group counts as done only when every question has an answer or "I'd rather not say".** Skipped questions show as "saved", not "done". Next waits for an answer. *(product, UX)*
- **"This matters a lot to me"** appears only on hopes and beliefs, not on facts such as having children, and only once an answer is chosen. The note nudging people to unmark answers is gone. *(behavioural)*
- **Set-up needs its answers:** first name, woman or man, where you live, church, your story, and at least one choice in each "open to" list. The Next button is disabled until then, with one quiet line saying why. *(product, UX)*
- **Back can't walk into set-up or a deleted account.** History is cleared when the ID check passes, when set-up ends and after a delete (`resetTo`). *(product, UX)*
- **A test strip on every screen:** "Test version · use made-up answers · nothing is saved". Untrue lines are fixed too: Sent no longer claims a report was sent, and "save as you go" is gone. *(product)*
- **Help on every tab screen** as a labelled button, with Close at the top of Get help now. *(UI, UX, pastoral, behavioural)*
- **Me is grouped** under My profile, Introductions, and Help and safety. Members can now change About me, My church and My story. There are rows for Still waiting and for Leave or delete my account. *(UX, pastoral)*
- **Introductions has three states:** not yet, paused, and waiting for Thursday. There is no "up to three" and no empty week before one is possible. Home opens the next question group in one tap, "Start again" is a quiet link, and Home says how long a pause is. *(behavioural, UX)*
- **Welcome leads with the companion:** "For marriage, and never one-to-one." The companion is now the first bullet. *(store, copy, marketing)*

## Decisions for the owner
1. **Covenant's colour** (brand reviewer, option A2): keep wine #7B2D3B in light mode, and use cream buttons with navy text in dark mode. The current dark pink is too close to KFL's rose for women (1.07:1), and a big pink button reads like a dating app.
2. **App icon:** round 1's concept A, a crown with two joined rings on navy. The rings would be light wine, never dark wine on navy.
3. **Store subtitle (30 characters).** The copywriter advises against "Faith first. Faces second. Never alone.", because single, widowed and divorced people can read "never alone" as a promise to end loneliness. The options are:
   - "Never one-to-one. Faith first." (copywriter)
   - "For marriage. With Companions." (brand)
4. **"Companion" or "companion"** on screen. The copywriter recommends the capital.
5. **A cap on "This matters a lot to me"**: none, 5, or 8. If there is one, it would be shown as a stated limit with "To mark this one, unmark another".
6. **When people invite their companion**: before introductions, or only before a conversation. Linked to this: what to offer people who have nobody to ask. The pastoral reviewer asks that nothing is promised on screen until that is decided.
7. **Whether same-day safety review runs on Sundays.**
8. **Whether a separated person can finish the questions and wait.**
9. **Waitlist plan** (marketing):
   - Groundwork January to March; public waitlist from Tue 6 Apr 2027.
   - Seven evenings hosted by churches.
   - About £1,500 budget.
   - London opens one church family at a time, with targets of ID-checked women and men in each family:

     | Church family | Women | Men |
     |---|---|---|
     | Catholic | 150 | 150 |
     | Eastern Orthodox | 60 | 60 |
     | Oriental Orthodox | 40 | 40 |
     | Anglican | 100 | 100 |
     | Protestant and free churches | 200 | 200 |

   - The waitlist page asks only for email, woman or man, area from a list, church family (with explicit consent) and an 18+ tick. The solicitor approves it first.

   Seven smaller decisions are at the top of marketing-2026-11-26.md.
10. **Book Apple's App Review appointment** (store review): Meet with Apple, Covenant's App Store Connect ID, the first slot after 15 Jan.
