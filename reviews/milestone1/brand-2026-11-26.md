# Covenant milestone 1: brand review (26 Nov 2026)

Brand designer. **Recommendations only.** The owner decides the logo, fonts and colours. I changed no app code and no colour file.

What I checked:
- the code: every screen in `app/`, `src/components/ui.tsx`, `helplines.tsx`, `src/theme/`, `src/lib/`, `app.config.ts` and the migration;
- the colours: `src/theme/colours.json` against `brand/colours.json` in the KFL repo;
- the screenshots: the contact sheets and single shots in `reviews/build/`;
- contrast: every text and control pair, worked out with the WCAG formula;
- a render of the accent options side by side, including KFL's rose (scratchpad swatch, not committed).

Ratings: **must** (fix before testers or the owner see it again), **should** (before milestone 2), **could** (when there is time).

---

## 1. The short verdict

The app already looks like a member of the Kingdom First family. It uses the same fonts, the same ink, muted and navy, the same calm cards and one filled button per screen. Prayer purple is used only for the prayer on "Before we begin". No screen uses gold. There are no hearts, no swipes and no counts. Every text colour passes WCAG AA in both light and dark.

These things are still wrong:
- there is no app icon or splash screen;
- the wordmark has no crown and is too small;
- the dark scheme reads pink, very close to KFL's rose for women;
- the toggle and field edges fail the 3:1 contrast rule for controls;
- the set-up screenshots are older than the code.

| Check | Result |

| Light neutrals (bg, surface, sunk, ink, muted, line, chip) | Match `kfl.light` exactly (#FFFFFF, #F3F0EA, #2A1840, #645A73, #E4DDEB, #ECE7DF). |

| Accent, tint, prayer | Match `covenant` and `covenant.dark` (#7B2D3B, #F4E7E9, #E4D8F3/#3F2163; #E4A6A0, #3A2533, #2E2747). |

Findings:

**C1 (should).** `brand/colours.json` › `covenant._note` says Covenant's neutrals "still use the old website values (ink 221636, muted 5E556C)". That is no longer true: the native app already uses the KFL app values. The `covenant` block also has no full `light` and `dark` token sets, so the "copied from" claim in `src/theme/colours.json` › `_source` can't be repeated by a script.
- **Fix:** add `covenant.light` and `covenant.dark` blocks to `brand/colours.json` with the exact keys the app uses, and update the note.
- **Then:** add a small check (in `check.yml`) that fails when `src/theme/colours.json` differs from those blocks.
- This needs the owner's OK, because it edits the family colour file.

**C2 (should).** `colours.json` has no tokens for these states:
- a focus ring;
- a disabled button;
- an error or warning edge. KFL avoids red in Care, and round 1 said to use "ink text with a wine left edge" for errors.
- **Fix:** add `focus`, `disabled` and `warnEdge` tokens now, so milestone 2's forms, the two yeses and the Companion pause states don't invent hex values.

## 3. Contrast (WCAG AA)

**Text: all pass.**

| Pair | Light | Dark |
|---|---|---|
| Accent text or link on page | 9.21 (#7B2D3B on #FFFFFF) | 8.95 (#E4A6A0 on #0F1424) |
| Accent on surface or card | 9.21 | 7.84 |
| Accent on selected tint | 7.65 | 6.88 |
| Button text on filled button | 9.21 (white on wine) | 7.84 (navy on wine-light) |
| Ink on selected tint | 13.39 | 12.67 |
| Muted on page | 6.46 | 7.75 |
| Muted on surface or sunk | 5.68 (sunk) | 6.79 |
| Muted on selected tint | 5.37 | 5.95 |
| Prayer text on prayer tint | 9.58 | 7.44 (#C9B2F2 on #2E2747) |
| Placeholder (muted) in field | 6.46 | 6.79 |

**Controls and edges: two fail the 3:1 rule for non-text (WCAG 1.4.11).**

**K1 (must). The toggle is off but can't be seen.** In `src/components/ui.tsx:56`, the off track is `p.line` with a `p.surface` knob.
- **What the person sees:** on light, the "This matters a lot to me" switch is a pale grey pill with a white knob on a white page: 1.33:1 for the track, and the knob is nearly invisible. On dark, the track is #2C345A on #0F1424: 1.52:1.
- **Why it matters:** this switch is the most important choice on the question screen.
- **Fix:** off track `p.muted` (6.46 light, 7.75 dark) with the knob in `p.surface`. Or keep `p.line` and add a 1.5px `p.muted` border. The on state is fine.

**K2 (should). Field and option edges are hard to see.** `Field` (`ui.tsx:184` onwards), the sign-in field (`app/signin.tsx:29`) and the Talk box (`app/talk.tsx:42`) use a `p.line` border.
- **The numbers:** 1.33:1 on light, and 1.33:1 against the dark surface.
- **What the person sees:** on light, the empty first-name and town boxes are faint outlines. In dark, they show only because their fill differs from the page, and that difference is only 1.14:1.
- **Fix:** a field-border token (#8F869C on light is about 3.4:1 on white; on dark, #636C93 is 3.13:1 on the surface #19203A and 3.58:1 on the page #0F1424). Add it to the colour file with the owner's OK.
- **Unselected options:** these don't need the change, because their radio or tick box is drawn in `p.muted` (6.46).

**K3 (could). In dark, the selected option shows mainly by its edge.** The selected tint #3A2533 against the card #19203A is only 1.14:1, so the selected state shows by its wine-light edge and the filled radio. That passes, because the edge is 7.84:1. But the tint's hue is 320°, the same family as KFL's rose, which makes the selected card look mauve. See A2 below.

## 4. Covenant's own accent: options and recommendation

I rendered each option on a Welcome-style panel: a button, a selected option, a link and the wordmark. KFL's God's Daughter rose and KFL White & purple sit beside them for comparison.

| Option | Light | Dark | For | Against |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Wine, as built** | #7B2D3B, white text | #E4A6A0 filled buttons, links, edges; tint #3A2533 | Strong meaning (Cana, "the new covenant in My blood"). Clearly not KFL purple. Every pair passes AA. Serious, not romantic, on light. | **On dark it reads salmon pink.** #E4A6A0 against KFL rose #E8A9CF is 1.07:1, nearly the same colour, and the tint's hue (320°) is rose's. A big pink filled button says "dating app". Wine on navy is 1.74:1, so the icon can never put wine straight on the tile. |
| **A2. Wine, with the KFL dark pattern** (recommended) | Same as A | **Filled buttons cream #F3EEE4 with navy text** (13.87:1). This is KFL's own dark accent on Today and Me (`kfl.dark.accentToday`). Wine-light #E4A6A0 only for the wordmark's "Covenant", links, the selected edge, tab underline and the toggle's on state. Selected tint warmer, #3A2628 (hue 354°; ink 12.71, wine-light 6.90, muted 5.97). | Keeps wine's meaning. Dark mode becomes calm and clearly family. The pink area on screen shrinks to thin lines and small text, so it no longer reads as KFL rose or "romance". | One new hex (#3A2628) for the owner to approve. Two button colours to keep in step (wine on light, cream on dark). |
| **B. Navy-led** | Navy #19203A buttons (16.04:1 with white). Wine only in the wordmark and icon. | Cream buttons | The most serious, institutional look. Strongest family link (navy is the icon tile). No romance reading at all. | Less of its own identity. Navy buttons on light look like many banking apps, and the app loses the colour that says "covenant". |
| **C. Deep green** (e.g. #2E5E4E) | 7.43:1 | — | Fresh, with no dating connotation. | Hue 160° sits right next to KFL's Renew teal (175–180°), and teal means Renew in the family. **Not recommended.** |

**Recommendation: A2.** Keep wine #7B2D3B as Covenant's colour on light, exactly as built. On dark, use cream filled buttons and keep wine-light for small accents only, with the warmer tint #3A2628. This needs one token change in the theme (a `primary` token separate from `accent` on dark) and the owner's decision.

**A1 (should).** Whatever the owner chooses, record it in `brand/colours.json` › `covenant` and in Covenant's CLAUDE.md the same day. Then remove "proposal" from `src/theme/colours.json` › `_source`.

## 5. Wordmark and icon

**W1 (must). There is no app icon or splash screen.** `app.config.ts` has no `icon`, `ios.icon`, `android.adaptiveIcon` or `splash`. Android test builds are planned from 15 Dec, and with nothing set they will show Expo's default icon. That is the same slip as KFL's early builds (CLAUDE.md: "builds before iPhone 9 / Android 100 had Capacitor's default icon").
- **Fix, once the owner picks the concept:** add `assets/icon-1024.png` (navy #19203A tile), an Android adaptive icon (foreground PNG plus background #19203A) and a splash in navy with the cream crown.
- **Concept:** round 1 recommended **A, crown and two joined rings with a gold base bar**. I still do. It is clearly KFL's sister at 29px, and the rings say "marriage" without hearts.
- **Wine on navy:** the rings must be wine-light #E4A6A0 or a mid wine of at least 3:1. Never #7B2D3B, which is 1.74:1 on navy.
- **Until then:** the build should not ship to testers with the default icon.

**W2 (should). The wordmark has no crown and is too small.** `Brand()` in `src/components/ui.tsx:63–71` sets "KINGDOM FIRST" in Cormorant 600 at **15px** with 2.1 letter spacing (.14em), and "Covenant" in Cormorant **500** italic at 22px. In the screenshots, "KINGDOM FIRST" is the smallest text on Home (`reviews/build/milestone1-2026-11-26-440-light.png`, Home) and looks like a footnote.
- **KFL's own app rule** (25 Nov review) is Cormorant at least 20px. 15px Cormorant caps are also thin on a 320 phone.
- **The family lockup is:** crown, then "KINGDOM FIRST" in caps, then the italic second word, all on one left edge.
- **Fix:**
  - add the crown (an SVG with `react-native-svg`, the plain crown from round 1 with no leaves, and the gold base bar as the family link) at about 28px tall, left-aligned;
  - "KINGDOM FIRST" at 20px with .09em tracking (about 1.8);
  - "Covenant" at 30px in **Cormorant 600 italic**, as round 1 proposed. Load `CormorantGaramond_600SemiBold_Italic` in `app/_layout.tsx:3,11–17`; today only 500 italic is loaded.
- **Accessibility:** keep `accessibilityLabel="Kingdom First Covenant"`.

**W3 (should). The full name isn't on every screen before sign-in.** The name in full is on Welcome only. Round 1 asked for the name in full on every screen people see before they sign in, plus "From Kingdom First Life." as the endorsement line.
- **Fix:** add the small wordmark to the top of Before we begin (`app/yes.tsx`) and Create your account (`app/signin.tsx`).
- **Endorsement line:** Me says "Kingdom First Covenant, from the makers of Kingdom First Life." (`app/me.tsx:27`) and Still exploring says "our sister app Kingdom First Life" (`app/exploring.tsx:10`). Pick one line, "From Kingdom First Life.", for Me, Welcome's foot and the store.

**W4 (could).** "KFC" appears nowhere on screen, which is good. Keep it out of file names that people see too. The URL scheme `kfcovenant` (`app.config.ts:10`) is internal, so it's fine.

## 6. Type

The fonts are right: Cormorant Garamond for headings, Plex for body, buttons and labels, and Cormorant italic for verses and the prayer. Plex is used at 14px or more everywhere, except eyebrows and verse references at 12px. Those are caps with tracking, as in KFL.

**T1 (should). There are two eyebrow styles.** `Eyebrow` (`ui.tsx:75`) is 12px with 1.9 tracking. The question screen draws its own: 13px with 1.6 tracking (`app/q.tsx:35`). Side by side, "MARRIAGE ITSELF" on a question doesn't match "INTRODUCTIONS" on the tab page.
- **Fix:** use `<Eyebrow>` in `q.tsx`.

**T2 (should). There are three heading sizes for second-level headings.**
- Still waiting's "A single life" is a `Title` at 26px with a 28 top margin (`app/waiting.tsx:12`).
- Marriage in Scripture uses `Label` (Plex 600, 15px) for its section heads (`app/scripture.tsx:10–19`).
- The question title is 27px (`app/q.tsx:36`).
- **Fix:** add one `Subtitle` style (Cormorant 600, 24px, line height 29) and use it for "A single life" and the Scripture section heads. Keep the question title at 27px as the one exception, because it is the page's main heading.

**T3 (should). "Tier 1" is internal language on screen.** It is the eyebrow on Faith and marriage questions (`app/questions.tsx:14`).
- **What the person sees:** a ranking word on the first screen of the questions, in an app that promises no scores.
- **Fix:** change the eyebrow to "About you" (as on the set-up screens), or drop it.

**T4 (should). The first group has two names.** It is "Your faith and church" (`src/lib/questions.ts:8`, and so the row and "Your faith and church: done"). But Home (`app/home.tsx:23`), Introductions (`app/intros.tsx:18`) and the questions intro (`app/questions.tsx:16`) say "Faith and church".
- **Fix:** use one name. "Faith and church" matches "Marriage itself" and reads better in "Faith and church: done".

**T5 (could). Verses are set in ink, not purple.** Verses are Cormorant italic in ink with a muted Plex reference (`ui.tsx:88–95`). KFL's website sets verses in purple with a gold reference. In Covenant, purple means prayer and gold means Live, so ink and muted are the right choice. **Keep it**, and write it down as Covenant's verse rule so milestone 2 doesn't "fix" it.

## 7. Consistency across schemes and screens

**S1 (must). The set-up contact sheets are older than the code.** `reviews/build/setup-2026-11-26-390-dark.png` has two problems:
- It shows "The teaching of all the churches in this app" under the marriage line. The code now says "The historic teaching of the Church, held today by the Catholic and Orthodox Churches and many others…" (`app/faith.tsx:16`).
- It shows a "Thank you … the questions aren't built yet" screen that no longer exists.
- **Fix:** re-shoot all four set-up sheets from the current build before the owner or the other reviewers rely on them. The pastoral D7 wording and the solicitor's check depend on seeing the real text.

**S2 (should). In dark, links, numbers and the selected state all share one pink.** In dark, every text link ("I'm still exploring", "Help and support ›", "If you're not safe at home: Get help now ›") and every phone number in Get help now is wine-light.
- **Why it matters:** on Get help now, the numbers are the one thing to find fast. Pink numbers beside pink buttons make the page feel urgent and "branded" rather than calm.
- **Fix with A2:** keep the numbers wine-light but make the buttons cream, so the numbers are the only colour on that page. On light, wine numbers (9.21:1) are good as they are.

**S3 (should). The delete button and the keep button look the same everywhere else.** "Yes, delete my account" is the same filled primary as "This is my yes" (`app/leave.tsx:23`).
- **Why it matters:** a filled wine button is close to "red danger" on light, and on dark it is the same pink as every happy action.
- **Fix:** with the C2 tokens, make the destructive confirm a secondary button with the `warnEdge` outline and ink text. "Keep my account" stays the quiet choice. Make it the same pattern KFL uses for "Delete what I saved".

**S4 (could). The screen headings don't share one top line.** The top margin of the Title jumps between screens: 40 on Checked, Exploring, Section done and Sent; 28 on Welcome; 22 on Home; 8 elsewhere. Screens without a Back link start lower than screens with one, so the heading moves when you go from Section done to Home.
- **Fix:** give every screen without a Back link a 48px spacer (the height of `Back`), so all titles sit on one line ("one layout grid").

**S5 (could). Help can't be reached from Home or Introductions.** Get help now is one tap from Welcome and Me, but Home and Introductions have no Help entry.
- **Fix:** a small labelled "Help" in the top right of the tab screens, as on KFL's Today. This is also a reach item for the UX reviewer: the safety line must be findable from every screen once conversations exist in milestone 2.

## 9. What milestone 1 must get right so milestone 2 fits

1. **A `primary` token separate from `accent` (should).** Milestone 2 adds the two yeses, the Companion states, photos and a season purchase. Each needs exactly one filled button per screen. On dark that button should be cream (A2) and the accent kept for edges and links. Split `Button kind="primary"` from `p.accent` now (`ui.tsx:127–141`), so the change is one line later.
2. **Companion identity without colour coding (should).** Never give men, women or Companions their own colours, and never pink and blue. Show a Companion as a name plus the word "Companion" in a neutral chip (#ECE7DF light, #222A47 dark), the same chip for everyone.
3. **The private safety line needs its own look (should).** "I feel pressured, or I'm worried about my safety" will live inside conversations. Give it the `warnEdge` style (C2): ink text, a 3px edge, never red, never prayer purple. Then it is recognisable on every screen and never mistaken for a prayer callout.
4. **Photos (could).** Profile photos sit on `sunk` with an 18px radius, no frames, no overlays and no filters. The brand look is round 1's: daylight, ordinary places, modest dress.
5. **Prayer stays purple (could).** "Pray for this introduction" uses `Prayer` (#E4D8F3 / #3F2163; dark #2E2747 / #C9B2F2). It never uses wine.

## 10. Store screenshots: what to show first

The Apple 4.3 review (`reviews/store/apple-4-3-review-2026-11-26.md`, points 1, 2 and section 1.4) says to lead with the one fact no other app has: no one-to-one chat, ever. I agree. In brand terms:

1. **Screenshot 1: a conversation with both Companions named on screen.**
   - Headline: "Every conversation includes a Companion for each of you."
   - Show the pause state "Paused: waiting for Ruth's Companion".
   - Navy frame, cream headline in Cormorant 600, small wordmark with the crown at the top.
   - No faces. This needs milestone 2 and seeded demo accounts.
2. **Screenshot 2: the faith summary, with no photo.** "Faith and marriage first." Light screen, wine accent.
3. **Screenshot 3: "Photos only when you both say yes."** Show the two quiet yeses, with no profile grid.
4. **Screenshot 4: safety.** The ID check and "Talk to us privately", with the forced-marriage help visible.
5. **Screenshot 5: the season.** "Your season: 6 months. It doesn't renew."
6. **Screenshot 6 (optional): Still waiting / A single life.** It shows the app's heart, but a reviewer will read it as softness, so put it last.

Rules for every screenshot:
- shot from the real build, light and dark;
- headline in Cormorant 600 cream on navy (#19203A);
- the device screen set into the frame;
- one left edge for all text;
- "dating" only ever as "not dating";
- no couples' faces, hearts, rings as jewellery or wine glasses.

**Subtitle (30 characters at most).** The comparison suggests "Faith first. Faces second. Never alone." It is too long, "Faces" puts looks into the headline, and "Never alone" can read as a promise of never being single.
- **Recommended:** "For marriage. With Companions." (30 characters). Name and subtitle then say what it is and what makes it different.
- **Alternative:** "Faith first. Never one-to-one." (30 characters).
- The owner decides. The pastoral reviewer checks.

What milestone 1 can show today: screenshots 2 (questions), 4 (safety) and 6 only. Hold the store set until milestone 2 has a seeded conversation.
