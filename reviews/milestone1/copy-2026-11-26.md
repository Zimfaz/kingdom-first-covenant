## 1. The positioning line

### "Faith first. Faces second. Never alone."

What works: it is short, it has rhythm, and "Faith first" is true and on brand (Kingdom *First*).

What doesn't:

| Part | Problem | Mark |
|---|---|---|
| "Never alone" | Read by a single person, a widow or someone divorced, it sounds like a promise that the app will end their loneliness. The pastoral study says Covenant must never imply that single people are incomplete or waiting for life to start (A9, "How should singleness be honoured?"). Welcome already says "You're not waiting to become complete." "Never alone" pulls the other way. | must not use |
| "Never alone" | It is also vague. A reviewer at Apple won't know it means "a Companion is always in the conversation". It could mean community, or even being watched. The store review says the Companion must be *seen* in the first 30 seconds. This line hides it. | must not use |
| "Faces second" | It makes looks the second test, as if faces were ranked after faith. Photos are not a test at all; they come after two yeses. It also reads a little flippant for a marriage service. | should not use |
| The whole line | It doesn't name the one structural difference the store review rests the case on: no one-to-one chat, ever (store review, summary item 1, and section "First 30 seconds"). | must |

### What to say instead

The plainest true sentence is: **every conversation includes a Companion for each of you.** "Never one-to-one" is the short form. It is concrete, a reviewer can check it, and it says nothing about loneliness.

**Store subtitle (Apple limit 30 characters):**
- Recommended: **"Never one-to-one. Faith first."** (30)
- Alternatives: "With a Companion, every time" (28); "Faith first, never one-to-one" (29).

**Store title:** "Kingdom First Covenant" (22). Don't stuff it.

**Promotional text / Google short description (first line):**
> Christian introductions for marriage, never one-to-one. Every conversation includes a Companion for each of you.

**First store screenshot line** (over a conversation that shows both Companions by name):
> **Every conversation has a Companion for each of you.**
> Small line under it: *A parent, friend, priest or pastor you trust.*

Screenshots 2 and 3, in the store review's order: "Faith and marriage questions first. No photos yet." / "Photos only when you both say yes."

"For marriage, not dating" stays, as the purpose line after the Companion line (store review, risks table). It is the purpose, not the difference.

### Welcome first line (`app/index.tsx`)

Today (line 9): "Introductions for marriage, not dating." Line 14 lists Companions last.

Proposed (must, with the pastoral reviewer's OK, as the store review asks):

| Where | Current | Proposed |
|---|---|---|
| Title, line 9 | Introductions for marriage, not dating. | **For marriage, and never one-to-one.** |
| Body, line 10 | We introduce Christians who hope to marry, slowly and with care, in conversations that always include a companion you trust. | Every conversation includes a Companion for each of you: a family member, friend, priest or pastor you trust. We introduce Christians in the UK who hope to marry, slowly and with care. Not dating. |
| Bullets, line 14 | Faith and marriage questions first / Photos only when you both say yes / Every conversation includes your companions | **A Companion for each of you, in every conversation** / Faith and marriage questions first / Photos only when you both say yes |
| Line 11 | Marriage is a good gift from God. So is a single life lived for Him. You're not waiting to become complete. | Keep, word for word. It is the best line in the app. |
| Line 12 | 1 Corinthians 7:7 | **should:** drop the verse from Welcome. It appears on three screens (Welcome, Marriage in Scripture, Still waiting) and pushes the bullets and Begin below the fold at 320. Keep it on the other two. |

If the owner prefers to keep "Introductions for marriage, not dating." as the title, then the body must open with the Companion sentence and the bullets must be reordered. Either way the Companion is in the first two lines.

## 4. What milestone 1 must get right so milestone 2 fits

1. **One word for the role** ("Companion"), fixed now, so M2's invite, conversation header and Companion sign-up don't inherit two spellings.
2. **The group names** are used in sentences elsewhere; fix the names now, because M2's "What you share / Things to talk about" will quote them.
3. **The open-to labels** must match the story labels word for word; M2 will show them on introductions.
4. **Welcome and the store** must say the same thing in the same order (Companion, faith first, photos after two yeses), because Apple compares screenshots with the app (2.3).
5. **"Today" promises** (Sent) must be set to what one person can keep, before conversations exist.
6. **Words to keep out**, carried from round 1 and the pastoral study: match, swipe, like, date (except "not dating"), chaperone, divorcee, "God has someone for you", "never alone", "Tier". None appear except "chaperone" (companion.tsx:11) and "Tier" (questions.tsx:14).
