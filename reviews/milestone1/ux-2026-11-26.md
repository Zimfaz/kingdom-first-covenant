# Kingdom First Covenant, milestone 1: UX review (26 Nov 2026)

UX reviewer. I read CLAUDE.md, round 1, the pastoral study, the comparison and the store review. Then I walked the real web export (the Expo build of 26 Nov, 00:56) in Chromium at 390×844, 360×780 with 130% text, and 320×568, all with isMobile. I tapped through every task and counted taps and scrolls. I also read every screen in `app/`, `src/components`, `src/lib` and the migration. I changed no app code.

My walk screenshots were throwaway files. The repo's screenshots are the reference: `reviews/build/setup-2026-11-26-*.png`, `reviews/build/milestone1-2026-11-26-*.png` and `reviews/build/shots-2026-11-26/`.

## Verdict

Milestone 1 is calm, kind and honest. It has no counts, scores or streaks, and the help routes are serious. The order of the screens is mostly right. The main problems are in the plumbing. The person can't find their way back to change who they are. Back goes to the wrong places. Set-up lets people through with empty or skipped answers. And two screens tell a tester something that isn't true ("we will look at this today"; a Report with no box to say what happened). All of these are cheap to fix now and expensive once milestone 2 builds introductions on top of them. Getting from Welcome to Home takes **47 taps on 28 screens** (13 of them questions). That is acceptable for a marriage app, because the questions are the product. The 18 taps of set-up before the questions can lose about 5.

What the person sees first, above the fold:
- **Welcome:** at 390 the whole page fits. At 320 and at 360 with 130% text, the person sees the headline, the purpose line and the verse, but not Begin (698px and 962px down). The Companion line is the third bullet.
- **Home:** "Hello, Ruth", one sentence about the next step, one button and the quiet word. Good.
- **Me:** eight rows of equal weight. Leave Covenant is below the fold at 320.

## Findings

Each finding gives the file and line, what the person sees, the fix, and **must / should / could**. "Must" means before milestone 2 builds on it, or before any outside tester.

### A. Navigation and Back

1. **Back from Home goes into set-up.** `app/secdone.tsx:16–17` and `app/questions.tsx:23` use `router.replace('/home')`, but everything below stays on the stack. In the walk, Back from Home opened the question list (`/questions`), then Open, Story and so on. On Android the back gesture does the same, so someone who has finished set-up can "back" into the ID check. **Fix:** when set-up ends, clear the stack (`router.dismissAll()` then `replace('/home')`), so Home is the root and Back leaves the app. Do the same after the ID check passes (`checked.tsx:11`): Back from About you must never reopen the ID screens. **Must.**
2. **After Delete, Back reopens the deleted account.** `app/leave.tsx:23` uses `replace('/')` on top of Home and Me. **Fix:** clear the stack, then open Welcome. **Must** (store review 3.6 wants deletion to look and act final).
3. **The tab bar comes and goes.** `Screen tab=` is set on Companion, Pause and Leave (`companion.tsx:8`, `pause.tsx:15`, `leave.tsx:11`). It is not set on Who I'm open to, My answers, Marriage in Scripture, Talk or Report, though all of these open from Me (`open.tsx:26`, `questions.tsx:12`, which shows it only once a group is done, `scripture.tsx:7`). The walk found the tab bar on 1 of the 8 screens reached from Me. **Fix:** one rule. Pages opened from Me show the tab bar with Me selected. Set-up, the questions themselves and Help (a modal) never do. **Should.**
4. **The Back label on a question lies.** `q.tsx:34` says "‹ Questions", but after the first question it goes to the previous question. The Android back gesture goes to the list instead, losing the place. **Fix:** label it "‹ Back" after the first question. Make the gesture behave like the button (keep `i` in the route, e.g. `/q?sec=D&i=3`). When someone comes back to a group, open the first unanswered question. **Should.**
5. **Help has Close only at the very bottom.** `help.tsx:25`: Close is 1,150px down at 390 and 1,420px at 360 with 130% text. Swipe-down works on iPhone, but nothing on screen says so. **Fix:** "‹ Close" at the top left, in the same place as Back everywhere else, and keep the bottom one. **Should.**
6. **Sign in says "Create your account".** Welcome's "I already have an account" (`index.tsx:17`) opens a screen titled "Create your account" (`signin.tsx:18`). **Fix:** "Sign in or create an account", or a `mode` parameter that changes the title. **Should.**

### B. Set-up lets people through with nothing

7. **Every Next works with nothing chosen.** Basics: no name and no "I am a" (`basics.tsx:18`; the walk went from an empty Basics to Faith). Church: no church (`church.tsx:30`). Story: no story (`story.tsx:34`). Milestone 2 can't introduce anyone without sex, church and story. **Fix:** keep Next tappable. If something is missing, show one quiet line above the button saying what is needed ("Choose your church family to go on."), and scroll to it. Never use red. **Must** (milestone 2 depends on it).
8. **"Who you're open to" checks only half.** `open.tsx:24` needs one marital-history tick but no church family. In the walk, one tick and Save went on to the questions. With no church family ticked, nobody can ever be introduced, and Home will say "No new introductions this week" forever. **Fix:** need at least one in each list, and say so in the line. **Must.**
9. **Save on Open looks active when it does nothing.** `open.tsx:39`: `ready && …` swallows the tap silently. The hint "Choose at least one" is easy to miss below 10 options. **Fix:** show the line next to the button on tap (as in 7). In set-up, label it "Next". "Save" only when it is opened from Me. **Should.**
10. **Skipping a whole group marks it "Done".** `q.tsx:26–29, 48`: "Skip for now" on the last question finishes the group. In the walk, six skips gave "Your faith and church: done", and the person counts as ready for introductions (`readyForIntros`). **Fix:** a group is "Done" only if each question has an answer or "I'd rather not say". Skipped questions stay open quietly: the list says "Whenever you're ready" (no count), and coming back opens the first skipped one. **Must** before milestone 2 matching.
11. **Three ways to move on from a question.** Next, "Skip for now" and "I'd rather not say" sit together (`q.tsx:46–50`), and Next with nothing chosen already skips. **Fix:** one button whose label follows the state: "Skip for now" until an answer is chosen, then "Next" or "Finish this group". Keep "I'd rather not say" as the one link. **Should.**
12. **"This matters a lot to me" works with no answer.** `q.tsx:41`: someone can mark a must-agree on a question they haven't answered. **Fix:** show the toggle only once an answer is chosen, and never with "I'd rather not say". Owner to set a limit (round 1: 5 or 8); today the nudge appears at 5 with no limit. **Should.**
13. **Separated is a link under the list, not a choice in it.** `story.tsx:33`. A separated person reading six choices may pick "I'm divorced" to get through. From Separated, "Go back" returns to Story, where they can carry on anyway (`separated.tsx:13`). **Fix:** add "I'm separated, not yet divorced" as the seventh choice. Choosing it shows the Separated words on the same screen and replaces Next with "Save and come back later". **Should.**
14. **The Church list is long.** `church.tsx:23–28`: 23 choices. Next is about 1,960px down at 390 and 2,360px at 360 with 130% text. **Fix:** keep all five group names visible as rows (Catholic, Orthodox, Anglican, Protestant and free churches, Others). Tapping one opens its list in place. This passes "a fold may hide length, never existence", because every family is on screen. Pin Next to the bottom of set-up screens. **Should.**
15. **The ID check comes before the person knows the questions.** `signin.tsx:38` → `whycheck` → `idcheck`. That is right for safety, but Why we check doesn't say what to have ready or how long it takes. **Fix:** add one line to `whycheck.tsx:13`: "You'll need a passport or driving licence. It takes about two minutes." **Could.**

### C. Honesty in the test version

16. **"Sent" promises a reply that will never come.** `sent.tsx:10` says "A member of the Covenant team will look at this today", but nothing is sent (`talk.tsx:43` says so only before sending). A tester who really is under pressure may believe it. **Fix:** with TEST_BUILD, say "Test version: this wasn't sent. If you need help now, use Get help now or call 999." **Must.**
17. **Report has no box.** `report.tsx` says "Tell us what happened" but offers only ticks. "Something else" with no words is useless to a moderator. **Fix:** add the same optional box as Talk. In milestone 1, open Report only from Help (as now), with the line "In milestone 2 you'll report from a person or message." In milestone 2, every person and message has Report and Block within two taps (store 3.1). **Must** (the box).
18. **Empty sends.** Talk and Report send with nothing ticked and nothing written (walk: `/talk` → Send → Sent). **Fix:** need one tick or some words. Explain in a quiet line. **Should.**
19. **Memory-only answers aren't explained.** A reload or a closed app forgets everything. In the walk, a reload gave "Hello" with no name. Store review 6 wants a visible build label. **Fix:** one quiet line on Home and Me while TEST_BUILD is on: "Test version: made-up answers, forgotten when you close the app." **Should.**
20. **An internal note on Leave.** `leave.tsx:17`: "How long reports are kept is for the solicitor to advise." This is a note for the team, not for the person. **Fix:** remove it until the solicitor gives the real period. **Should.**

### D. Home and Introductions

21. **Home sends people to a list, not to the next group.** `home.tsx:24` → `/questions`, so it takes 3 taps to the next question. **Fix:** "Continue: Marriage itself" opens `nextNeeded()` directly (as `secdone.tsx` does). Once the needed groups are done, add a quiet line "More questions, whenever you're ready ›" for the optional groups. **Should.**
22. **"See introductions" opens the same empty message.** `home.tsx:28–30` says "No new introductions this week" and offers a button to a page that says the same. **Fix:** in milestone 1, drop the button. In milestone 2, Home shows the Thursday's introductions or nothing, and names the next Thursday by date ("Your first introductions could come on Thursday 3 December"). The comparison and the store review both say real members should never meet an empty week. **Should.**
23. **The Introductions tab title contradicts its body before the questions are done.** `intros.tsx:13` says "No new introductions this week" to someone who can't have any yet. **Fix:** before ready: "Introductions start after two groups of questions", with Continue. **Should.**
24. **A pause has no end on screen.** `pause.tsx` stores "two weeks", but Home only says "Your introductions are paused" (`home.tsx:21`), and the migration has `paused boolean` only. **Fix:** "Paused until Thursday 10 December" on Home and in Me's note. In milestone 2, store `paused_until timestamptz`, and decide what a pause does to an open conversation (I suggest the Companions see the same "paused" as any pause, with no reason). **Should.**

### E. Me: the reach check

25. **No way to change About you.** Name, town, church, parish and story are asked once and never again. Me has no row for them (`me.tsx:19–26`). Someone whose declaration of nullity comes through can't move from "in process" to "declared null", and their introductions stay wrong. **Fix:** a first row "About me" (name, town, church, story so far) that reuses the set-up screens with Save. **Must.**
26. **Me is eight rows of equal weight.** **Fix:** three plain labels, with the rows in task order:
   - **My profile:** About me, My answers, Who I'm open to, My companion.
   - **Introductions:** Pause introductions.
   - **Help and safety:** Get help now, Talk to us privately.

   Then Marriage in Scripture, and last "Leave or delete my account". **Should.**
27. **Deletion is hidden behind "Leave Covenant".** Apple 5.1.1(v) and the store review want deletion easy to find. **Fix:** rename the row "Leave or delete my account". Keep Pause first on that page. **Should.**
28. **Sign out, Privacy and Terms are missing.** Store review 6 lists Privacy and Terms for milestone 1. **Fix:** a quiet foot in Me with Privacy · Terms · Sign out. **Should.**
29. **Reach check, part by part:**
    - **Get help now:** everyone. Reached from Welcome before sign-up, from Story for divorced people, from Separated, Sent and Me. Good.
    - **Talk to us privately:** every member. Reached from Me and Help only. In milestone 2 it must also be inside every conversation (owner decision), and it must be easy to find without anyone looking over the person's shoulder.
    - **Marriage in Scripture:** only from Me. **Could:** make Home's quiet word open it once a week.
    - **Still waiting:** only from the Introductions tab and Leave. Fine.
    - **My companion:** from Me with "Chosen later". This is the app's whole difference (store review 1.3), yet a new member first meets it in the third Welcome bullet and then not again until milestone 2. **Should:** move it to the first Welcome bullet (store 2.3) and add one line to Home after the questions: "Before your first introduction: who could be your companion? ›".
    - Nothing important sits behind a fold except the church teaching on Open. That fold is right, because it hides length, not existence.

### F. What milestone 1 must get right so milestone 2 fits

30. **Consent before special-category data.** The `consents` table has `religion` and `marital_history` (migration lines 33–41). No screen asks for consent, but Faith, Church and Story collect exactly that. **Fix:** one plain consent card on the Faith screen (religion) and one on Story (marital history), each with Yes / Not now, stored with the wording version. Without them, milestone 2 can't save a real answer. **Must.**
31. **The data model is behind the screens.** `save_profile` saves only name, sex and town. Church, story, who someone is open to, answers, must-agree answers and pause length have no columns. Build them with the screens' exact values (`Story`, the `CHURCHES` strings, question keys like `D3`), and give the questions stable ids instead of `section.k + i` (`q.tsx:17`). Otherwise reordering a question later silently moves people's answers (the same trap KFL has with Life seasons list positions). **Must.**
32. **When the companion is invited.** Today the plan is "when a conversation is about to start" (`companion.tsx:25`). That puts the slowest step (an invitation plus a second ID check) at the most eager moment, and the other person waits. **Recommend** offering the invitation right after the needed questions ("Invite now, or when your first introduction comes"). This is a decision for the owner (below). **Should.**
33. **Messages back from the team.** Talk says "We usually reply the same day" (`talk.tsx:29`), but there is nowhere to read a reply. In milestone 2, add a quiet "From the Covenant team" row in Me. It is never pushed onto Home, never counted, and reached only with the passcode or face lock if one is added. **Should.**
34. **A quick exit on Talk and Help.** Pressure from family often happens with the family in the room. Talk and Help have no quick way out. In milestone 2, after the safeguarding adviser: a "Leave quickly" link that opens Home. Note that the question list must not reveal anything either. **Could** now, a decision for the adviser.
35. **Pin the main button on long set-up screens.** Next or Save is below the fold on Church, Story and Open at every size. Begin and "This is my yes" are below the fold at 320 and at 360 with 130% text. On the Leadership question, Next is below the fold at 360/130%. A bottom-pinned button (inside the safe area) on set-up screens fixes all of them. The question screens mostly fit, so keep them as they are. **Should.**

## Questions for the owner

1. **Companion invitation:** right after the needed questions (the person can still choose "later"), or only when the first conversation is about to start (today)?
2. **Must-agree answers:** a firm limit of 5, of 8, or only the gentle nudge as now?
3. **Separated:** should a separated person be able to finish the questions and wait, or stop at "Save and come back later"?
