I did not edit any app code. Milestone 2 features (introductions, yeses, Companions in conversation, photos, season, engagement) are not counted as missing.

**Marks:** **must** = fix before testers or before milestone 2 starts; **should** = fix before the Android test build (15 Dec) or as the next step; **could** = when there's time.

## Summary

1. **Must:** "Skip for now" on a group's last question marks the whole group **Done** (`app/q.tsx:26-29, 48`). Someone can skip every question in the two needed groups and Home says they're ready for introductions. Matching on empty answers would break the whole promise of "faith first".
2. **Must:** answers are stored by list position (`key = section.k + i`, option indexes). Any wording change by the pastoral reviewer or the tradition readers that adds, removes or reorders a question or option will silently move people's saved answers to the wrong question. Give every question, option and church a stable id now, before anything is saved on the server.
3. **Must:** set-up lets people go on with no sex, no first name, no church, no marital story and no church family ticked. Man–woman introductions, the teaching fold and matching all depend on these.
4. **Must:** the navigation stack keeps every set-up screen under Home, and under Welcome after "Delete my account". Android back and the iPhone swipe take people from Home back into "You're checked", ID and the faith statement, and from Welcome back into a deleted account's Me.
5. **Must:** test wording isn't all behind `TEST_BUILD`, and `TEST_BUILD` is a hard-coded `true`. "Your answers save as you go" and "A member of the Covenant team will look at this today" are untrue in this build. Testers aren't told on the set-up screens to use made-up answers, so real religion and marital history may be typed in.
6. **Must (server, before milestone 2):** the base migration lets a new account skip the sign-ups switch (`is_test` defaults to true), has no way to create a profile row, stores pause as yes/no when the app offers "two weeks / a month", and has no way to give or withdraw consent.
7. **Should:** the Companion, Covenant's one real difference (store review), is the third bullet on Welcome and otherwise lives only in Me. Get help now is missing from Home. Nothing in set-up speaks to someone whose family is creating the account for them.
8. **Should:** the church families in "Who you're open to" don't match the church list. Oriental Orthodox is folded into Orthodox, and "Another church" or "between churches" members can never be chosen by anyone.
9. **Should:** before milestone 2, decide and build what matching needs and milestone 1 doesn't ask: an age range, a structured place (not free text) and a cap on "This matters a lot to me".
10. **Build list to 4 Dec:** below (section 7). About 4 working days of Covenant time, after each day's KFL work, and nothing after 3 Dec.

## 4. Server schema and RLS: what the base migration must fix, and what milestone 2 needs

### 4.1 `20261125234900_cov_base.sql`, already applied (new migration, applied with the owner present)
- **Must, line 27:** `is_test boolean not null default true`. Once any path creates profile rows, every new real account is a test account. `save_profile` (line 65) then lets it through while sign-ups are off. Default **false**; only an admin function sets true.
- **Must:** nothing creates a `profiles` row. `save_profile` only `UPDATE`s, so it updates nothing, with no error. Add an `on auth.users insert` trigger (security definer) that creates the row with `is_test = false`, or upsert inside the function.
- **Must, line 28:** `paused boolean`. Use `paused_until timestamptz` plus `paused_open boolean` ("until I start again"). Introductions skip both. Nobody but the member can read either (pastoral C7: a Companion is never told).
- **Must, lines 34-44:** consents can't be given or withdrawn (all writes revoked, no function). Add `give_consent(kind, wording_version)` and `withdraw_consent(kind)`. Withdrawing religion or marital history **deletes** the matching answers in the same transaction. The `kind` check lacks **`biometric`** (the ID selfie, before Stripe Identity) and **`companion_reads`** (the member agrees their messages are read by both Companions). Sexual orientation implied by man–woman introductions is for the solicitor and the DPIA to name.
- **Should:** `save_profile` doesn't check `adult_checked` (the plan says "18+ checked in the function"). Every member write function should refuse unless `adult_checked or is_test`.
- **Should:** don't let `sex` change by a plain call once a person has had an introduction. Changes go through support.
- **Could:** `set search_path = ''` and schema-qualified names in security-definer functions. A retention rule for `audit_log` (solicitor).

### 4.2 `_cov_faith` (planned; write it now, apply later)
- `traditions(id text pk, family text, name, sort)`. One list, used by both Your church and Who you're open to. Families: Catholic, Eastern Catholic, Eastern Orthodox, **Oriental Orthodox** (split, pastoral C5), Anglican, Protestant and free churches, Other. Every church maps to exactly one family, and every family can be ticked, so nobody is unreachable. Today `TRADS` (`open.tsx:11`) has 4 families and the church list has 5 groups.
- `questions(id text pk, section, sort, text, note, needed, version)` and `question_options(question_id, id text, text, sort)`. Read-only content. Ids never reused.
- `answers(user_id, question_id, option_ids text[], rather_not_say bool, must bool, updated_at, pk (user_id, question_id))`, with `check (not must or (not rather_not_say and cardinality(option_ids) > 0))`. Written only by `save_answer()`. That function needs a live religion consent and `adult_checked`, and enforces the must cap.
- `marital(user_id pk, story)`. Needs marital-history consent.
- `openness(user_id pk, histories text[], families text[], age_min, age_max, area …)`. Own rows only.
- No member ever selects another member's rows. "What you share" comes only from a function returning words, never scores (plan, round 1).

### 4.3 `_cov_safety` (planned; write it now, apply later)
- `safety_requests(id, user_id, kinds text[], body, urgent, status, created_at)` and `safety_replies(request_id, from_staff bool, body, at)`. Member: own rows only. **No function a Companion can call ever joins these.** Replies reach the member **inside the app**, never by email or text by default. In forced-marriage cases a family member may read the email (the "one chance" rule, pastoral C7). The talk screen must then say where the reply will appear.
- `reports(id, reporter, subject_user, intro_id, message_id, reasons, body)`, `blocks(blocker, blocked)`. A block excludes both people from each other's introductions for good.
- `moderators(user_id)`. Moderator functions check `auth.jwt()->>'aal' = 'aal2'` (two-step sign-in).
- The urgent alert reuses KFL's urgent-alert pattern, on Covenant's own project.

### 4.4 What milestone 2 needs from the shape chosen now
- **Members and Companions are different accounts.** Add `profiles.role ('member' | 'companion')`, or a separate `companions(member_id, user_id, status, checked_at, removed_at)`. A Companion account can't create a member profile (their promise: "not looking for marriage here myself"). Removing a Companion is quiet, and leaves the conversation paused, never one-to-one.
- `introductions` plus `introduction_members(intro_id, user_id, yes_at, closed_by_me)`. A partial unique index allows only one open conversation per member. Each side's yes can be read only by that side. Closing shows the same "This introduction has closed" to everyone (pastoral C7).
- `messages(intro_id, author, body, at)`. Writes only through `post_message()`, which refuses unless a checked, active Companion for **each** member is present, and filters numbers, emails, links and addresses. No two-person table (store review: the case to Apple).
- `seasons(user_id, product, starts, ends, source)`. Written only by the RevenueCat webhook (service role), never by the app.
- Photos: a private bucket at `user_id/…`, signed links from a function only after two yeses.
- Engagement: each person confirms separately and privately. The account then goes to a quiet engaged state for at least 6 months, keeping Help and Talk (pastoral C8).
- Delete: an Edge Function with the service role. It deletes the auth user (cascade), closes open introductions kindly for the other person, pauses conversations where the person was a Companion, and keeps only what the solicitor sets (reports, the ID result).

## 5. What milestone 1 must get right so milestone 2 fits

- **M1 (must): stable ids** for questions, options, churches and families (B6, 4.2). This is the one change that gets more expensive every day it waits.
- **M2 (must): routing by state.** Route groups and an index redirect (B10), so "invited Companion", "season ended" and "engaged" become new states, not new pushes.
- **M3 (should, owner decides): matching inputs nobody asks for yet.**
  - **An age range.** Age comes from the ID, but there's no preference.
  - **A structured place.** Town is free text (`basics.tsx:17`). It can't be matched or opened area by area (the comparison's London waitlist), and it invites addresses. Use a list of UK towns or postcode districts, keep the district private, and show the town.
  - **How far they'd go.** "Would you move" sits in the optional Everyday life group.
  - **The must-agree cap** (B5).

  Decide all four before the server tables are written.
- **M4 (should): consent steps in the flow now, as screens only.**
  - Terms (including "no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users", Apple 1.2 and Google UGC) at sign-up.
  - Religion, before Your church.
  - Marital history, before Your story so far.
  - The ID selfie (biometric), before the ID check.

  In the test build each screen says nothing is recorded. Adding them later means reshuffling set-up and re-shooting screenshots.
- **M5 (should): "What others will see".** A read-only preview in Me of the faith summary another person would see: words only, no scores. People need it before the first Thursday, and it's the screen the store screenshots and reviewers will look at first.
- **M6 (should): an honest Home for each state.**
  - The first Thursday hasn't come yet ("Your first introductions could come on Thursday 10 Dec").
  - No one this week (today's wording).
  - Not open in your area yet (the waitlist).
  - Paused.

  Store review: the reviewer must never see an empty app. The demo seed solves that for review, and these states solve it for real members.
