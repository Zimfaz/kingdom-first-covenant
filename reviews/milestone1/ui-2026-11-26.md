# Kingdom First Covenant, milestone 1: UI review (26 Nov 2026)

Reviewer: UI. Scope: layout, type scale, spacing, contrast in light and dark (WCAG AA, `src/theme/colours.json`), touch targets, the tab bar, large text and small phones, plus the reach check. I didn't edit any app code.

## How I checked
- I read every screen in `app/`, plus `src/components/ui.tsx`, `src/components/helplines.tsx`, `src/lib/onboarding.tsx`, `src/lib/questions.ts` and `supabase/migrations/20261125234900_cov_base.sql`.
- I looked at the contact sheets in `reviews/build/` and the single screens in `reviews/build/shots-2026-11-26/`.
- I re-rendered the current web export at 320×568 light, 360×740 at 130% text, 390×844 dark and 440×956 dark. I measured with getBoundingClientRect: the back link, tab bar, help-line rows, primary buttons, switch, Close and sideways scroll.
- I computed the contrast for every pair in `colours.json`.
- The web export has no safe areas, no keyboard and no native font scaling. So the findings on those come from the code and need checking on a phone.

## What already works
- **Text contrast passes AA everywhere, in both schemes.**

  | Pair | Light | Dark |
  |---|---|---|
  | ink on page | 16.1 | 16.5 |
  | muted on page | 6.5 | 7.8 |
  | muted on raised (sunk) | 5.7 | 6.0 |
  | accent on page | 9.2 | 9.0 |
  | button text on accent | 9.2 | 7.8 |
  | ink on selected tint | 13.4 | 12.7 |
  | prayer ink on prayer tint | 9.6 | 7.4 |

  Placeholders use muted, so they pass too.
- **No sideways scroll** at any width I tested.
- **Touch targets meet 48px** on buttons (52), rows (56), choices (52), tabs (56), text links (48) and phone numbers (48 or more).
- **The tab bar fits.** Three equal tabs: 107px wide at 320 and 147px at 440. "Introductions" stays on one line at 360 with 130% text.
- **Big phones use their width.** At 440 the content is 400px wide, with 20px sides like the smaller phones.
- **No counts or progress bars.** Light purple appears only on the prayer card in *Before we begin*.

## Findings

### Must

**M1. The forced-marriage phone number runs out of its card at 360 with 130% text.**
- Where: `src/components/helplines.tsx` lines 12–14, used inside the card in `app/talk.tsx` line 36.
- What the person sees: on *Talk to us privately*, after ticking "My family or someone else is pushing me to marry", "020 7008 0151" ends at x=350. The row ends at x=323 and the card border is at x=340. The number crosses the padding and the border. In the same card, "Karma Nirvana, free, Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm" is squeezed to five lines of one or two words. The same squeeze shows on *Get help now* ("England (24 / hours)", "Wales, Live Fear / Free").
- Why it's a must: this is the safety screen for the most important safeguard.
- Fix: give each help line two lines. Put the name on the first line at full width. Put the number on the second line in accent semibold, with no right column. Keep the whole row as one 48px or taller target. This also lines the numbers up like a table, on one left edge, at every width. Milestone 2's in-conversation safety line can reuse the same layout.

**M2. Get help now can't be reached from Home or Introductions.**
- Where: `app/home.tsx` lines 11–13, `app/intros.tsx` line 11 and `app/me.tsx` line 23.
- Reach check:
  - Who it's for: anyone who feels unsafe, including someone under family pressure in their first week.
  - Where they find it: on Welcome (a text link), and then only as the fifth row in Me.
  - Home and Introductions have no Help.
- Fix: add one header component to every tab screen, with the brand on the left and a labelled "Help" button on the right (48px or more, opens `/help`). This copies Kingdom First Life's Today header. Milestone 2 needs the same header on every conversation screen ("Get help now on every conversation screen", `reviews/store/apple-4-3-review-2026-11-26.md` line 154). Build it once now so the conversation screens inherit it.

**M3. The "This matters a lot to me" switch can't be seen when it's off.**
- Where: `src/components/ui.tsx` lines 56–57.
- What the person sees:
  - Light: a white knob on a #E4DDEB track, on a white page, at 1.3:1.
  - Dark: a #19203A knob on a #2C345A track, at 1.3:1. The knob nearly disappears.
- Why it's a must: WCAG 1.4.11 asks for 3:1 for the parts of a control. Without it, people can't tell the switch is there or that it's off. It is the main control on every question.
- Fix: draw the off track with a 1.5px muted border (6.5:1 light, 7.8:1 dark). Make the off knob muted rather than surface. Keep the on state as it is (accent track, surface knob).
- No new colour is needed; this only uses existing tokens.

### Should

**S1. The tab bar and the home bar on Face ID iPhones.**
- Where: `src/components/ui.tsx` lines 14–17.
- What the person will see: the SafeAreaView adds the bottom inset below the tab bar and paints it in `bg`. In dark that is #0F1424, under a #19203A tab bar, so on every Face ID iPhone a 34px band of a different colour sits under the tabs.
- Fix: for tab screens, leave out the `'bottom'` edge. Give the tab bar `paddingBottom: insets.bottom` (`useSafeAreaInsets`), so its surface runs to the bottom of the screen.
- Check on a real iPhone that the tab bar sits above the home bar on all 12 sizes listed in the KFL CLAUDE.md.

**S2. Tapping the Me tab does nothing on Me's own pages.**
- Where: `src/components/ui.tsx` line 21, plus `companion.tsx`, `pause.tsx`, `leave.tsx` and `questions.tsx`, which pass `tab="me"`.
- What the person sees: on *My companion*, *Pause* or *Leave*, the Me tab is highlighted, but tapping it does nothing (`!on && …`). People expect a highlighted tab to take them back to its start.
- Fix: when the tab is on but the route isn't the tab's start, `router.replace(href)`.
- Also, `app/questions.tsx` line 12 shows the Me tab whenever one group is done, even when the person came from Home's "Continue the questions". Either show no tab bar there, or pass the tab they came from.

**S3. Help needs Close at the top.**
- Where: `app/help.tsx` lines 18–26 and `app/_layout.tsx` line 25.
- What the person sees: *Get help now* opens as a modal, and its only Close is at y=1215 on a 568px-tall screen, more than two screens down.
- Fix: put a "Close" text button (48px or more) at the top right, next to the title.
- On iPhone, also check the modal sheet. The `'top'` safe-area edge inside a page sheet can add an empty band of about 50px. Drop `'top'` for modal screens if it does.

**S4. The keyboard covers the fields, and the first tap only closes it.**
- Where: `src/components/ui.tsx` line 15 (`ScrollView`); fields in `signin.tsx`, `basics.tsx`, `church.tsx` and `talk.tsx`.
- What the person will see:
  - There is no `KeyboardAvoidingView`, so on *Talk to us privately* the box and "Send to the Covenant team" sit under the keyboard.
  - There is no `keyboardShouldPersistTaps="handled"`, so on *Sign in* the first tap on "Email me a code" only closes the keyboard.
- Fix: wrap Screen in `KeyboardAvoidingView` (`behavior="padding"` on iOS), and set `keyboardShouldPersistTaps="handled"` and `keyboardDismissMode="interactive"` on the ScrollView.
- Milestone 2's conversation box depends on this.

**S5. Large text is tested only in the browser.**
- Where: no text has `maxFontSizeMultiplier`; the test is `reviews/build/covshot3.cjs`.
- The risk:
  - The 130% check uses the browser's `text-size-adjust`. React Native uses the phone's font scale instead, and iOS accessibility sizes reach about 310%.
  - At 200% and above, "Introductions" (14px) is wider than its 107px tab at 320. React Native will then break the word mid-way.
- Fix:
  - On the tab labels only, set `maxFontSizeMultiplier={1.6}` and `numberOfLines={1}` with `adjustsFontSizeToFit`.
  - Leave body text free to grow.
  - Check on an Android emulator at font scale 1.3 and 2.0, and on iOS at the largest accessibility size, before testers.

**S6. Text boxes have no visible edge in light.**
- Where: `src/components/ui.tsx` line 195, `signin.tsx` line 29 and `talk.tsx` line 42.
- What the person sees: a white field on a white page with a #E4DDEB border, at 1.33:1. "First name", "Where you live", the email box and the "Tell us more" box barely show as fields. WCAG 1.4.11 asks for 3:1.
- Fix: make field borders muted (1px) or a stronger line token.
- This is a colour change, so **ask the owner** before adding a new token. Using `muted` needs no new colour.
- The same 1.33 line on unselected choice cards is acceptable, because the radio or tick inside each card is muted at 6.5:1.

**S7. Button has no disabled or destructive style.**
- Where: `src/components/ui.tsx` lines 127–142; `app/open.tsx` line 39; `app/leave.tsx` line 23.
- What the person sees:
  - On *Who you're open to*, Save looks ready but does nothing when nothing is ticked (`ready && …`).
  - On *Leave*, "Yes, delete my account" is the same filled wine button as Begin and Continue.
- Fix:
  - Add `disabled`: muted fill, `accessibilityState={{ disabled: true }}`, and the "Choose at least one" note announced.
  - Add `kind="danger"`: an outlined button in ink, with no red, so deleting never looks like the next step.
- Milestone 2 needs both: a disabled Yes until the summary is read, and "Close this introduction".

**S8. The church list is one 2,000px page.**
- Where: `app/church.tsx` lines 23–28.
- What the person sees: 25 choices under five headings, with Next at y=2028 at 320. A Quaker or someone between churches scrolls past 23 rows first.
- Fix: show the five family rows first, each opening its own list on the same screen. The headings always show, so "a fold may hide length, never existence" holds. Alternatively, put Next in a footer that stays pinned at the bottom.

**S9. Begin is below the fold at 320.**
- Where: `app/index.tsx` lines 9–18.
- What the person sees: on an SE (1st gen), Begin is at y=682 on a 568px screen, so the first screen shows no action. On *Before we begin*, "This is my yes" starts at y=568, exactly off screen.
- Fix: at widths under 360, reduce the title's `marginTop: 28` to 12. Then either move the three-point card below Begin, or keep the card and drop the 1 Corinthians verse to after the buttons.
- The store review asks for the Companion point to lead Welcome anyway (line 122), so reorder the card in the same change.

**S10. The type scale is too fine-grained, and the smallest labels are 12px.**
- Where: `ui.tsx` lines 75, 93 and 80, and `q.tsx` lines 35–36.
- What I found:
  - Eleven sizes are in use: 12, 13, 14, 15, 16, 18, 19, 22, 26, 27 and 32.
  - Eyebrows and verse references are 12px uppercase muted. Kingdom First Life raised dates and references to 13px after its 25 Nov review.
  - `q.tsx` builds its own 13px eyebrow and a 27px title instead of using Eyebrow and Title.
- Fix:
  - Add a `type` token to `src/theme/index.ts` with: label 13, small 14, body 16, lead 18, verse 19, h2 26, h1 32.
  - Use Eyebrow in `q.tsx`.
  - Make Title take a `size="h2"` prop rather than inline font sizes.
  - Set reference and eyebrow letter-spacing to 1.6.

**S11. The setup contact sheets are out of date, and three screens were never shot.**
- Where: `reviews/build/setup-2026-11-26-*.png`.
- What I found:
  - The sheets still show the removed "Thank you" screen ("the questions aren't built yet") and the old faith note ("The teaching of all the churches in this app").
  - Welcome, Before we begin and Sign in are in neither 26 Nov set.
- Fix: re-shoot the setup sheets from the current build with those three added, and with Talk shown in both states. Before showing the owner, check the shots with a fake inset (top 47/59, bottom 34).

### Could

**C1. Space between the title and what's above it varies.**
- Where: `home.tsx` line 13 (22), `index.tsx` line 9 (28), and 40 in `checked.tsx`, `exploring.tsx`, `secdone.tsx` and `sent.tsx`.
- Fix: one value. Use `space.xxl` (28) for screens with no Back link, and none after Back.

**C2. The Me screen is one flat list of eight rows of equal weight.**
- Where: `app/me.tsx` lines 19–26.
- Fix: group the rows under three small labels: **My profile** (My answers, Who I'm open to, My companion), **Help** (Get help now, Talk to us privately, Marriage in Scripture) and **Introductions** (Pause introductions, then Leave Covenant last, set apart).
- The name card shows "Your name" in Cormorant on a bordered card, which looks like a text box. Use plain text with no border.

**C3. The Introductions note is a bordered card holding one muted line.**
- Where: `app/intros.tsx` lines 22–24.
- What the person sees: it reads like an empty placeholder box.
- Fix: plain small text, no card.

**C4. Radios aren't grouped for screen readers.**
- Where: `basics.tsx`, `church.tsx`, `story.tsx` and `pause.tsx`.
- Fix: wrap the choices in a `View` with `accessibilityRole="radiogroup"` and an `accessibilityLabel`, as `q.tsx` line 38 already does.

**C5. The dark accent is a light rose.**
- The dark accent #E4A6A0 (round 1's "light wine") colours every link, tab and button in dark. In Kingdom First Life, rose is kept for women's private spaces.
- This is a brand point, so it is **for the owner, not a change**. The light-wine proposal is still undecided (`CLAUDE.md`). Decide it before store screenshots.

**C6. Marriage in Scripture is only reachable from Me.**
- Fix: add a quiet "Marriage in Scripture ›" link under Home's *A quiet word* card.
- This goes beyond the reach check's must, because it isn't a safety feature.

## Milestone 1 must get these right so milestone 2 fits
1. **One header and one help-line layout** (M1, M2): conversation screens need Help and the private line on every screen.
2. **Keyboard handling and a footer that stays at the bottom** (S4, S8): the conversation box and the two-yes buttons need them.

4. **Tab-bar insets and large-text limits** (S1, S5): set them before more screens copy the pattern.
