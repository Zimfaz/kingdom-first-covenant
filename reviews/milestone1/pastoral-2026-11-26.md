## 3. Findings

**F1. must. `app/exploring.tsx:9`.** The screen says "…introduces people for a Christian marriage that every main church can bless." That isn't true. A Catholic and Orthodox couple needs permission or economy. Many churches will not bless a marriage after divorce. It makes the same mistake as the old faith line. Change to:
> Kingdom First Covenant introduces Christians who hope to marry. If you're still exploring faith, we're really glad you're asking.

**F2. must. `src/lib/questions.ts:26`, the leadership note.** It ends "If anyone uses Scripture to frighten or control you, please tell us." But the question screen gives the person no way to tell us. Show a "Talk to us privately ›" link under any note that asks people to tell us. In `q.tsx`, this could be a `link` field on Q. Without it, the promise is empty for the very person it is written for.

**F3. could. `app/faith.tsx:18`.** Matthew 19:6 is the first verse a divorced member meets, one screen before "Your story so far". Use Genesis 2:24 here instead, and keep 19:6 on Marriage in Scripture, as the study suggested.

**F4. should. `app/leave.tsx`.** Someone may be made to leave, for example by a family that is forcing a marriage or by a controlling partner. Add this above "Pause instead":
> Is someone making you leave? Talk to us privately ›

Also, line 17 shows an internal note to members: "How long reports are kept is for the solicitor to advise." Replace it with:
> Your profile, answers and conversations will be deleted. We keep reports for a limited time, to keep people safe.

The solicitor sets the time. Line 14, "Leaving without a marriage isn't failing", assumes why the person is going. Change it to "If you're leaving without a marriage, that isn't failing."

**F5. should. `app/open.tsx:11, 13`.**
- Split 'Orthodox' in TRADS into "Eastern Orthodox" and "Oriental Orthodox (Coptic, Ethiopian, Eritrean, Armenian, Syriac, Malankara)". The two families aren't in communion with each other, and their marriage rules differ.
- Add to the end of the Catholic text: "Nobody is turned away from the process because they can't pay."
- **K:** if the member's church is Catholic and their story is 'process', add: "Your priest may advise waiting for the decision before you begin a new relationship."

**F6. should. `app/open.tsx` under Church families (study C6).** Add one honest line:
> Marrying across churches can be a great gift, and it asks for patience. Talk to your priest or pastor early, especially about where you'd marry and how you'd raise children.

**F7. should. `questions.ts:9`, the baptism question.**
- The Salvation Army and Quakers are on the church list, and neither baptises with water. Add the option "My church doesn't baptise with water".
- Change "Yes, I was received or chrismated" to "Yes, and I was received or chrismated into my church". Chrismation and reception aren't baptism; they follow it.
- Do the same for Holy Communion (line 17). Add the option "My church doesn't celebrate it with bread and wine".

**F8. should. `questions.ts:17`, the note on Holy Communion.** It says "There are no wrong answers here, only honest ones." Catholic and Orthodox readers will hear that as saying doctrine doesn't matter. Change to:
> Christians answer these differently, and your church's teaching matters. Answer honestly; nothing is scored.

**F9. should. `questions.ts:22`, "Marriage is…".** Orthodox Christians and the Church of England's Common Worship both say "holy mystery". Change the first option to "a sacrament (a holy mystery)".

**F10. should. `questions.ts:34–36`, children.** This question can hurt people who can't have children, or who have lost a baby. Add this note to line 34:
> If having children may not be possible for you, you don't need to explain. "I'd rather talk about it" is always fine.

Skip line 36 (baptising children) when the person has answered "I'd prefer not to". Add this note to line 36 (CIC can. 1125):
> If one of you is Catholic, the Catholic partner is asked to promise to do all they can to have the children baptised and brought up in the Catholic Church.

**F11. should. `app/home.tsx:29` and `app/intros.tsx:15`.**
- Home says "We only introduce people we believe you could marry with a clear conscience." Only the member can judge their own conscience. Change to: "We only introduce people who fit what you've told us matters most."
- Intros says "…than introduce you to the wrong person." That hints at "the right one". Change to: "…than introduce you to someone who doesn't share what matters most to you."

**F12. could. `app/scripture.tsx:13`.** "When Rebekah's family were asked about her marriage" goes further than the text, as study A2 notes. Change to:
> When the time came for Rebekah to leave home, her family said, "We will call the girl and ask her opinion." She said, "I will go." (Genesis 24:57–58). Nobody can choose for you.

**F13. could. `app/scripture.tsx:18`.** End Ephesians 5:25 with "…", because the sentence goes on into verse 26.

**F14. could. `questions.ts:13`, the note on fasts.** Change to "Many Orthodox, Catholic and Anglican homes keep fasts and feasts together…". Anglicans keep Lent too.

**F15. could. `questions.ts:10`, worship.** Add the option "When I can (illness, work or caring)". Without it, people who are ill, on shifts or caring for someone may feel judged.

## 6. What milestone 1 must get right so that milestone 2 fits

1. **The member's own yes.** Nothing in milestone 2 should let a companion, parent or priest answer, say yes, see photos first or close for the member. The data model should hold no "act on behalf" role.
   - **should:** say on sign-in, now: "This account is yours. Nobody else may set it up or use it for you." The comparison found that parents create 25–50% of Shaadi.com accounts.
2. **Reports and Talk to us are invisible to companions.** Build the reports table so that no companion can ever read it, through row level security and no joins. Make the closed-introduction view the same whoever closed it and for whatever reason (study C7).
3. **People with nobody to ask.** Companion.tsx:25 assumes everyone has a family member, friend, priest or pastor. Decide before milestone 2 how converts, estranged, widowed and newly arrived members get a companion (round 1 §5.6; volunteer companions from partner churches). Until then, don't promise it on screen.
4. **The confessor line** (study C3) goes on the invite screen: "If your companion is your confessor, what you say here isn't confession, and they may prefer to keep the two apart."
5. **Engagement and closing** (study D4, D5): Colossians 4:6 on closing; each person confirms engagement privately; Help and Talk to us stay open for at least 6 months afterwards.
6. **Consent screens.** The `consents` table records religion and marital history. The words people agree to, shown before the faith and story screens, need my check and the solicitor's.
7. **The remarriage question (questions.ts:27) and "Who you're open to"** must never conflict. "Who you're open to" governs introductions. The question only informs the conversation. **could.**
