How it works

Four rules shape every introduction

1

Faith first

Introductions come from answers on faith and marriage. No scores or percentages.

2

Photos second

Shown only after both say yes to the faith summary, to both at the same moment.

3

Never alone

Each person brings a companion: family, a friend, a priest or a pastor.

4

Slow by design

Up to three introductions a week, on Thursday evening, never on a Sunday.

Catholic, Orthodox, Anglican and Protestant Christians are welcome. Each member chooses which churches they are open to.

Kingdom First Covenant · for executives

With no companion present, a conversation pauses. One open conversation at a time keeps it focused.
