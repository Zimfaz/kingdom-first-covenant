The problem and the opportunity

Christians who want to marry are poorly served by today’s apps

The problem

  • Apps are built for endless swiping, not commitment.
  • Faith is a profile tick-box, not the starting point.
  • Strangers talk one-to-one; safety rests on the user.
  • Churches and families have no place in it.

The opportunity

An app plainly for marriage, with shared faith first and accountability built in, can earn the trust of churches, families and members.

That trust is the product, and it is hard for others to copy quickly.

The gap is not more choice; it is trust. Covenant is built around marriage, faith and accountability from the first screen.
