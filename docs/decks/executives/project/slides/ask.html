Risks and what we need

The risks are known, and so is the help we need

Key risks

  • Law: a Christians-only, man–woman service needs a solicitor’s view (Equality Act, Online Safety Act, UK GDPR).
  • Safety and trust: one serious incident would hurt the brand, so moderation is human and companions are built in.
  • App stores: Apple and Google look closely at introduction apps.

What we need

  • Introductions to churches willing to try it with their members.
  • A solicitor with Equality Act and online safety experience.
  • A safeguarding adviser and a second trained moderator.
