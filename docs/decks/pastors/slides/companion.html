Where a pastor or priest fits

A companion walks with each person

A companion can

  • Be a parent, a friend, a pastor or a priest, invited by the member.
  • Read the conversation and take part in it.
  • Pause it, and report anything that worries them.

A companion cannot

  • Say yes or no for the member, or close an introduction.
  • Message anyone privately.
  • Tell either person what God wants them to decide.

Companions pass the same ID check as members and never pay. If a companion is missing, the conversation pauses.

Kingdom First Covenant · for pastors and priests

A member can change their companion quietly at any time. The companion’s promise includes: “I won’t tell either of them what God wants them to decide.”
