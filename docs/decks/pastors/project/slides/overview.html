In development · we would value your counsel

Helping Christians who hope to marry meet one another, with family, friends and the church beside them.

Not a dating app. Shared faith comes first, photos only after both say yes, and no conversation ever happens one-to-one. Single life is never second best.

“Plans fail for lack of counsel, but with many advisers they succeed.”

Proverbs 15:22 (BSB)

✓A companion can

Be a parent, friend, pastor or priest the member invites; read and take part; pause and report.

✕A companion cannot

Say yes or no for the member, message anyone privately, or say what God wants them to decide.

A sister app to Kingdom First Life · November 2026

The companion is wise counsel, not a command. Companions pass the same ID check, never pay, and if one is missing the conversation pauses. A member can change companion quietly.
