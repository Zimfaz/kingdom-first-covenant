# Kingdom First Covenant: round 1 brief for the reviewers (23 Nov 2026)

Read CLAUDE.md in this folder first. **Nothing is built until the owner approves.** Each reviewer answers from their own expertise, plainly and briefly, and ends with the decisions the owner must make.

## The owner's request (his words, lightly tidied)
"The app is called Kingdom First Covenant and is a Christian marriage app. It should have two-tier matching: tier 1 based on answers to theological questions, including those on marriage, and then pictures. The intention should be clear that this is only for marriage and not dating; the purpose of the app is to introduce people so they get married, not to waste time dating. Make it cross-tradition and include divorced and annulled, because Catholics do not permit divorcees but permit annulled. Keep all the reviewers, give me a prototype and help with the launch."

## Decided (owner, 23 Nov)
- Sister brand with its own app and store listings, run by Kingdom First Life Limited; shares the reviewers and the family look.
- UK only at launch.
- After a match, conversations are **chaperoned only**: a family member, friend or pastor is always part of the conversation.
- Reviewers' brief first, then a clickable prototype.

## Questions for the reviewers
1. **Purpose and promise.** How the app makes "for marriage, not dating" real in every step (not only in words): intent pledge, pace, limits on matches at once, timelines, what ends a match kindly.
2. **Tier 1: theology and marriage questions.** What to ask, how many, how answers become a match (must-agree vs nice-to-have), how to keep it fair across Catholic, Orthodox, Anglican, Protestant and other traditions, and what each person can set as non-negotiable (e.g. tradition, children, where to marry, roles, faith practice).
3. **Marital history.** Never married, widowed, divorced, annulled (decree of nullity), divorced with an annulment in process. How each person says what they are open to; how the app explains Catholic and Orthodox teaching without shaming; what proof (if any) is asked.
4. **Tier 2: pictures.** When photos are revealed (after Tier 1? both say yes?), how many, modesty rules, verification selfies, no filters, no swiping.
5. **Chaperoned conversations.** How a person invites a chaperone, what the chaperone sees and can do, what happens if no chaperone is available, how meetings in person are arranged safely (public places, with family), and how a match moves towards engagement or ends.
6. **Safeguarding and law (UK).** Adults only and age assurance; identity verification; romance-scam and abuse protections; the Online Safety Act 2023 duties for a user-to-user service; UK GDPR special category data (religion, marital history, photos); the Equality Act 2010 and who can be matched with whom (Christian marriage between a man and a woman across the traditions; the solicitor must advise); moderation and reporting; data deletion when people marry or leave.
7. **Behaviour.** No swiping, streaks, badges, "likes" counters or infinite feeds; how the app creates hope without anxiety, rejection without shame, and celebrates engagements without pressure.
8. **Product.** Must-haves for a first release vs later; data model; verification and moderation tools; pricing (free and paid, honest, no pay-to-be-seen); store rules for dating apps (Apple and Google); effort and cost; risks.
9. **Market (UK).** Competitors (Christian dating and matchmaking apps and services), who it is for, positioning, channels (churches, parishes, Orthodox communities), launch plan and costs.
10. **Brand.** A sister identity to Kingdom First Life: name use, wordmark idea, colours and tone (proposals only; the owner decides).
